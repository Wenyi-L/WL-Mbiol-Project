Many come from Jones et al. (2008, Ecology Letters) which says: 

  The way recruitment was estimated varied among populations. Where possible it was defined 
  as the number of offspring produced between the census at time t and the census at time t+1 
  that were still alive at the census at time t+1. (<-------- this would be f_x)  
  Where this information (number alive at time t+1) was not available, we used the number of 
  weaned (mammals), or fledged (birds), offspring.  (<-------- in between m_x and f_x). 

In the list below, anything from Jones et al. 2008 is characterized as f_x.   

Humans, Japan and Sweden: not clear, the cited data base gives both raw births and "fertility" (more like f_x).
